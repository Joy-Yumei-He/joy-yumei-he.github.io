01 / RESEARCH

Research Overview

I examine how AI capabilities are internalized as human, organizational, and scientific capabilities, and how institutional arrangements and learning processes shape these outcomes.

01

Platform Design

Information provision, recommender systems, and matching mechanisms, with an emphasis on user privacy, participation, and behavioral responses.

Related research ↗
02

AI for Science

AI agents and open science platforms for construct operationalization, experimental design, computational evaluation, and scientific discovery.

Related research ↗
03

Human-AI Co-evolution

Behavioral and organizational mechanisms of AI adoption and human-AI co-evolution, including their implications for learning, work, and the distribution of benefits.
